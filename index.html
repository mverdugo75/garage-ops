The 4-Week Strength Wave

Same 4-week wave drives the primary lift on strength days. Reps drop, weight climbs, then it deloads and resets 5–10 lb heavier.

WEEK 1 12 · 10 · 8 · 6 3 RIR
WEEK 2 10 · 8 · 6 · 4 2 RIR
WEEK 3 8 · 6 · 4 · 2 1 RIR
DELOAD 4 × 4–6 50–60% Wk 3

↺ Week 5 restarts the wave, 5–10 lb heavier than Week 1

Gear Guidelines

Jogging Stroller

Primary tool for Zone 2 days. The added rolling resistance keeps your HR elevated while pacing remains conversational.

Ruck

Reserved for weekend active recovery walks or light post-lift walks. Builds work capacity without joint impact.

Weighted Vest

Best used for push-ups, pull-ups, or calisthenics finishers on upper body days. Avoid wearing on heavy squat days.
