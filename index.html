Notes
Direct chest/shoulder/back/arm volume drives the physique side, while Bench, Deadlift, Squat, and OHP stay as the strength backbone. Carries and core work live in the Finisher — real function without eating into the volume that actually builds a visible physique.

Weighted vest: use it on Finishers and accessory work once those feel too easy. Skip it on the four main barbell lifts — add weight to the bar instead.

Physique comes down to body fat percentage as much as training — this builds the muscle, diet reveals it.
