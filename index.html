5-Day Functional Fitness

GARAGE OPS

Barbell · Trap Bar · Curl Bar · Pulley · Rack · DB 20/30
Progression
Squat, Deadlift, Bench, OHP: add weight once you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, cleanly. These are the four numbers worth tracking closely.

Carries: fixed 20/30 DBs mean load caps out fast — progress with distance instead once it feels controlled the whole way. Trap bar can still go heavier as grip and core adapt.

Conditioning day: track rounds/time — more work in the same window over weeks is the goal, not just finishing.
Notes
All four majors get a full dedicated day — real strength focus, not squeezed in as an accessory. Trap bar and the pulley carry the functional-pattern work (carries, rotation, anti-rotation) that a straight strength split skips.
