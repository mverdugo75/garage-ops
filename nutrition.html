5-Day Family Protocol & Gram Precision Dashboard

05:30 AM — Pre-Workout Fuel

50g Cream of Rice (Dry Weight) + 30g Whey Protein + 60g Banana 320 kcal | 28g P | 48g C | 3g F

06:00 AM — Garage Ops Training

30–40 oz Water + Pinch of Sea Salt

07:30 AM — Post-Workout Recovery

350g Non-Fat Greek Yogurt + 30g Whey + 75g Blueberries + 30g Sliced Almonds 480 kcal | 50g P | 35g C | 18g F

12:30 PM — Midday Fuel

200g Grilled Marinaded Chicken Breast + 225g Roasted Sweet Potatoes + 150g Green Veggies 550 kcal | 48g P | 50g C | 14g F

06:30 PM — Family Dinner

185g Ground Beef + 120g TJ's Autumnal Harvest Sauce + 170g Cooked Pasta + 150g Broccoli 650 kcal | 53g P | 60g C | 22g F

09:00 PM — Bedtime Recovery

150g Low-Fat Cottage Cheese + Cinnamon 200 kcal | 28g P | 8g C | 4g F

Your Dinner Plate (Target: ~650 kcal)

  • Ground Beef: 185g
  • Autumnal Harvest Sauce: 120g (~1/2 cup)
  • Cooked Pasta: 170g (~1 cup cooked)
  • Roasted Broccoli: 150g

Wife's Dinner Plate

  • Ground Beef or Chicken: 130g
  • Autumnal Harvest Sauce: 120g
  • Cooked Pasta: 130g
  • Roasted Broccoli: 100g

Baby's Soft Plate

  • Finely Diced Protein: 40g
  • Soft Autumnal Pasta: 50g (cut into small pieces)
  • Soft Sweet Potato / Broccoli: 50g

Sunday Batch-Prep Station

  • Bag in Citrus Herb or Italian marinade for at least 30 mins.
  • 5–6 mins per side at 400°F direct heat. Rest 10 mins before slicing.
  • Season with garlic, onion, salt, pepper across two skillets.
  • Roast 6 cubed sweet potatoes + 4 bags broccoli until tender.
  • Boil 1 kg dry pasta al dente; store lightly oiled for quick dinner assembly.
